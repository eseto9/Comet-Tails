How to play

Peg (cyan ring) — hold to swing around it
Stardust (gold sparkle) — fly through it to collect
Prism (pink) — worth 3 stardust
Corona (dashed ring) — graze it to bank your tail for points
  • Hold (Space / tap / any gamepad button) to grapple the nearest anchor star and orbit it. Orbiting builds speed.
  • Release to fling off in a straight line. Timing is everything.
  • Fly through stardust to grow your tail. Golden prisms are worth 3.
  • Graze the sun's glowing corona to bank your tail. Payout is triangular: a tail of n pays 1+2+…+n. Long tails pay hugely — but don't touch the white-hot core.
  • Anchors overheat if orbited too long (shared between everyone!).
  • Color sets: stardust comes in gold, ember and nebula. Bank a tail that's 75%+ one color (5+ motes) for ×1.5, or 100% one color (8+) for ×2. Prisms take your tail's main color.
  • Unstable dust (flashing red, spawns near the corona and edges) is worth 5 — but it decays out of your tail after 8 s unless you bank.
  • Perk draft (solo / co-op): after every wave pick 1 of 3 upgrades. Build safe or greedy.
  • Peg ownership (versus): orbit a peg for 1 s to paint it your color. Rivals overheat your pegs twice as fast, and every lap around your own peg adds +1 mote.
  • Bounty (versus): the leader wears a 👑. Slicing their tail pays you half of what it was worth, instantly.
  • Anything crossing your tail severs it and scatters the dust. Asteroids do it. Rivals do it on purpose.
  • Solo: 3 hulls. Bank a tail of 20+ to repair one. Waves escalate; rogue comets hunt your tail.
  • Co-op Comet Train: teammates within the dashed link beam bank together as ONE tail — 10 + 10 pays 210, not 110. Shared hulls, no friendly fire.
  • Daily Constellation: the same seeded sky for everyone today. Your best run is replayed as a white ghost.
  • Versus: most banked when time runs out wins. The final 20 s is a Supernova: corona grows, payouts double.
