Field guide

How to play

Peg — hold to swing around it. Orbiting builds speed.
Stardust — fly through it to grow your tail. Three colors: gold, ember, nebula.
Prism — worth 3, takes your tail's main color.
Corona — graze it to bank your tail. Never touch the core.
  • Release to fling in a straight line. Let go at top speed for a Slingshot.
  • A tail of n banks for 1+2+…+n. Long tails pay hugely — but anything crossing your tail severs it.
  • Near misses: skim a rock (+1), the sun's core or a black hole (+2) without dying.
  • Color sets: 75%+ one color (5+ motes) banks ×1.5, a single color (8+) banks ×2.
  • Unstable dust (red) is worth 5 but decays out of your tail after 8 s.
  • Map events roll in every ~30 s: meteor showers, dust blooms, peg blackouts and solar flares.
  • Solo / co-op waves: each wave shows its objective at the top — bank the required stardust to clear it, then draft a perk. Every 5th wave is a boss: slice the Devourer's tail, or outlast the Singularity.
  • Versus: orbit a peg for 1 s to own it (rivals overheat it 2× faster; you earn +1 per lap). The leader carries a bounty. The final 20 s is a Supernova (×2).
  • Co-op Comet Train: linked teammates bank as one tail. Shared hulls, no friendly fire.
